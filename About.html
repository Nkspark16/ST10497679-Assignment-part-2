Perfect Plumbing provides professional plumbing installation, maintenance, repair, and emergency services. Our services can include:

  • Plumbing installations
  • Plumbing repairs and maintenance
  • Leak detection and repairs
  • Burst pipe repairs
  • Blocked drains and drain cleaning
  • Geyser installation and repairs
  • Hot- and cold-water pipe installations
  • Bathroom and kitchen plumbing
  • Tap, toilet, basin and shower installations
  • Drainage systems
  • Plumbing renovations
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Plumbing inspections
  • Emergency plumbing services
Where required, services can be tailored to the specific requirements of the property and project.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES:


Perfect Plumbing will aim to: Complete all plumbing work to a high professional standard. Use suitable, quality plumbing materials. Comply with applicable plumbing standards and regulations. Complete the work within the agreed project timeframe. Maintain a clean and safe working environment. Minimise disruption to the client and occupants. Communicate clearly with the client throughout the project. Identify potential plumbing problems and recommend appropriate solutions.

SCOPE OF WORK:
The proposed plumbing works will include:

Inspection:


A detailed inspection of the existing plumbing system will be conducted to identify existing problems, required repairs, and any potential issues.

Installation and Repairs


Perfect Plumbing will carry out the required plumbing installation and repair work according to the agreed scope of work.

Testing:


Upon completion, the plumbing system will be inspected and tested to ensure that the installed or repaired components are functioning correctly and that there are no visible leaks.

Completion:

:
The work area will be cleaned and handed back to the client upon satisfactory completion of the project.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT:


Perfect Plumbing will supply the necessary plumbing materials and equipment as agreed in the quotation.
Materials will be selected according to the requirements of the project, durability, compatibility, and applicable standards.
Any additional work or materials that fall outside the original scope will be discussed with and approved by the client before proceeding, where reasonably possible.
